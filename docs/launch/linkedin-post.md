Co-op season produces a lot of spreadsheets.

Rows of companies, roles, dates, and a status column that slowly fills with "Rejected", "In review", and the occasional "Interview!!". By the end it's a pretty good record of how much work a search takes — and almost nobody ever sees it.

So I built **Co-wrapped**: paste your application tracker and it turns into a short, animated recap of your season — your volume, your busiest week, where things stand, and a search persona — plus a card you can share.

A few things I cared about:

🔒 **Your spreadsheet never leaves your browser.** No account, no upload. Company names are hidden on cards by default, and you choose every stat that appears.

📊 **Honest numbers.** A current status isn't treated as your whole history, applications without a reply are "no recorded response" (not "ghosted"), and rates always show their denominator.

🧩 **Messy sheets are fine.** It detects your columns, normalizes statuses like "rejected 😭", flags duplicates and ambiguous dates, and lets you fix anything before the story plays.

If you're mid-search: every one of those rows took real effort, offer or not. It's worth seeing it all in one place.
