Format: vertical 1080×1920 for Reels/TikTok/Stories, plus a 16:9 crop for X/LinkedIn. Record on a phone-sized browser (390×844) with real Google Sheets data of fictional companies (use the `optimist` or `marathoner` sample text from `src/lib/samples/data.ts`, pasted into a sheet). Captions burned in; no voiceover needed.
